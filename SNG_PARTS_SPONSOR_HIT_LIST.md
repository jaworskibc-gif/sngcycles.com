# SNG Parts Sponsor Hit List

Goal: get useful parts support for Unit 001 without sounding like a free-stuff beggar.

Core pitch:

SNG is building Unit 001 publicly. A sponsor part is not just installed. It gets explained, filmed, tagged, credited in the spec sheet, and shown to riders/builders who care about serious electric supermoto parts.

## Priority Parts

1. Battery / pack
   - Why it matters: biggest cost, biggest buyer question, most important spec.
   - Ask: battery sponsorship, discount, or partial credit.
   - Content value: battery install, spec explanation, safety/BMS discussion, ride result.

2. Motor / controller
   - Why it matters: powertrain is the build's identity.
   - Ask: motor/controller sponsorship, discount, or tech support.
   - Content value: why this setup, tuning, torque, speed, reliability.

3. Brakes
   - Why it matters: the bike needs serious stopping power.
   - Ask: caliper/rotor/master cylinder sponsorship.
   - Content value: before/after brake feel, safety angle, why brakes matter on heavy/high-power e-moto.

4. Wheels / hubs
   - Why it matters: supermoto stance and strength.
   - Ask: wheel set, hub support, or discount.
   - Content value: fitment, stance, tire setup, supermoto conversion.

5. Fork / suspension / adapters
   - Why it matters: full-size feel and 6'7 rider story.
   - Ask: fork sponsorship, rebuild support, adapter kit, discount.
   - Content value: ride height, geometry, why it does not feel like a toy.

6. Tires
   - Why it matters: recurring cost and visible in content.
   - Ask: tire sponsorship or dealer support.
   - Content value: grip, street/lot testing, burnout/ride content.

7. Bars / controls / cockpit
   - Why it matters: rider feel, tall rider fit, visible details.
   - Ask: bars, grips, controls, levers.
   - Content value: cockpit setup, fitment, controls walkthrough.

8. Wrap / graphics
   - Why it matters: instantly visible, content-friendly, merch alignment.
   - Ask: wrap/graphic sponsorship or design discount.
   - Content value: before/after reveal, brand identity, gold/orange/white evolution.

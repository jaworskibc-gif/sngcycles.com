## THE FORMULA (every post obeys this)
**1. HOOK (first 1.5 sec)** — a punch in the face. A number, a claim, or a flex. They decide to keep watching HERE.
**2. TENSION** — the problem, the struggle, the "nobody else does this."
**3. PAYOFF** — the reveal / the proof / the gold bike.
**4. CTA** — numbered units, Florida-built, reserve yours, link in bio.

If a post doesn't have all 4, it's not done.

## THE VOICE (how SNG always sounds)
- Blue-collar, no fluff, a little cocky, earned-not-given.
- Short punchy lines. Lowercase energy ok. One emoji max: 🦈
- Always lands on: **Built not bought · No gas hauls ass · Florida-built, VIN-stamped · Shark Not Goldfish**
- Never corporate. Never "we are excited to announce." Talk like texting your boy.

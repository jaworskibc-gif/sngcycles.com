## 2. 420 vs 520 chain — and “520E” naming

### Don’t confuse the words

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **270-E @ 27 kW** | Old power class |
| **520E @ 52 kW** | New power class (~double) |
| **420 / 520 chain** | Chain **pitch** size — **not** the bike name |

**You do not need 520 chain just because the bike is called 520E.**

### At 52 kW + 14/54

| | |
|--|--|
| **Load** | ~2× the old 27 kW stress on the same gearing |
| **14/54** | Still fine as a starting gear — expects **hard launches** |
| **420 Heavy Hitter + good RK 420** | OK to **run and prove** if line/slack/alignment are perfect; inspect often |
| **520 chain** | Extra strength margin — switch if 420 **stretches, pinks, or breaks** under full power; needs full sprocket/kit change |

**Rule of thumb:** Stay on quality **420** until it proves weak at 52 kW. Don’t buy 520 only because of the “520” in the model name.

## SECTION 6 — Comment Templates

**Rule:** These must pass the "would a real rider say this" test. Never sound like a brand.

---

**Template 1 — Electric Performance Enthusiasm**
*(Use on: first-ride reactions, dyno results, acceleration clips)*

"That launch is exactly what gas riders don't expect. Instant torque at zero RPM changes what 'fast' means on a supermoto. The handling conversation always comes after — once you ride it you stop caring what's powering it."

---

**Template 2 — Build Quality Appreciation**
*(Use on: custom build reveals, fabrication process, small brand spotlights)*

"The detail work on that frame is serious. You can tell someone spent time with that — the kind of thing you lose when production scales too fast. Small-batch building is the only way to keep that level of craft."

---

**Template 3 — Technical Engagement**
*(Use on: spec discussions, "is it fast enough" debates)*

"Real question — what's the weight distribution with the battery pack? On a supermoto the balance point matters as much as the power number. Curious how it feels through tight corners vs the power delivery on the straights."

---

**Template 4 — Lifestyle Alignment**
*(Use on: riding lifestyle content, sunrise/sunset rips, garage life)*

"This is the version of riding I want every morning. No warmup, no fumes in the garage, just twist and go. Gas bikes are fun but the ritual of electric is something else."

---

**Template 5 — Sur Ron Upgrade Path**
*(Use on: maxed-out Sur Ron builds, "what's next" threads)*

"You've already done the work — that Sur Ron is worth more in mods than some complete bikes. At some point the question becomes whether you keep stacking upgrades on the platform or step into something built from the ground up for that performance level. Genuinely curious where you go next."

---

**Template 6 — Community / Ride Event**
*(Use on: group rides, event coverage, regional meetups)*

"That's a solid turnout for a mid-week ride. The electric group at the last local ride was small but had the most interesting machines. The conversation is different — people actually know what they're riding and why."

---

**Template 7 — Natural SNG Surface (USE SPARINGLY)**
*(Only when someone directly asks "who's building X" or "where can I find a custom electric supermoto")*

"There's a small builder in Florida called SNG Cycles doing exactly this — Shark Not Goldfish, hand-built electric supermotos. Not mass production, not a kit bike. I've been watching their build process and the attention to detail is real. Worth a look if you're serious about the category: @SNGCycles"

---

**Template 8 — Ducati/KTM Migration**
*(Use on: Hypermotard, Duke, or SMC content where riders ask about electric alternatives)*

"The Hypermotard's chassis dynamics are hard to argue with — that's still the benchmark for supermoto feel. The question I keep coming back to is what that geometry and weight balance looks like with an electric drivetrain. The torque curve would be completely different. Someone's going to get that right eventually."

## SECTION 7 — Accounts to AVOID

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| Commuter e-bike channels (RadPower, Lectric) | Wrong buyer — optimizing for price, not performance |
| Teen Sur Ron content (14–22 audience) | Wrong conversion profile, brand misalignment risk |
| Beginner "first motorcycle" communities | Not a first bike — price objections, no conversion |
| Long-distance EV touring accounts | Range objections SNG isn't built to answer |
| Gas-bike hardliner / anti-EV accounts | Troll traffic, damages brand, zero convertible buyers |
| Mass-market scooter/moped communities | Wrong lifestyle, wrong budget tier |
| Commercial fleet EV accounts | B2B, wrong product entirely |
| Under-18 motorsport content | Wrong lifecycle stage, zero near-term ROI |
| RC car / drone / EV hobbyist communities | Adjacent but wrong product category |

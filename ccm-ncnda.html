This Non-Circumvention, Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date above between Complete Care Marketing, LLC d/b/a SNG Cycles / Shark Not Goldfish Cycles ("Company") and the Recipient identified above (each a "Party," collectively the "Parties").

1. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship relating to the design, development, manufacture, marketing, and sale of custom electric motorcycles and related products under the SNG Cycles / Shark Not Goldfish Cycles brand (the "Purpose"). In connection with the Purpose, Company may disclose Confidential Information (as defined below) to Recipient. This Agreement governs all such disclosures.

2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any and all non-public information disclosed by Company to Recipient, in any form or medium, including but not limited to:

  1. Proprietary product designs, engineering specifications, and build processes — including the convertible rear-end quick-change assembly system and any related mechanical innovations (the "Quick-Change Rear System");
  2. Supplier identities, pricing, terms, and sourcing relationships;
  3. Investor identities, deal structures, term sheets, and financial arrangements;
  4. Customer lists, prospect lists, and reservation or deposit holder information;
  5. Business plans, financial projections, unit economics, and marketing strategies;
  6. Intellectual property, including trade secrets, patent applications (filed or unfiled), trademarks, and copyrights; and
  7. Any information that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature and circumstances of disclosure.

Confidential Information does not include information that: (i) is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement by Recipient; (ii) was rightfully known to Recipient prior to disclosure; (iii) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction; or (iv) is independently developed by Recipient without use of any Confidential Information.

3. Non-Disclosure Obligations

Recipient agrees to: (a) hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without Company's prior written consent; (c) use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose; (d) limit access to those of Recipient's personnel who have a need to know for the Purpose and who are bound by obligations no less protective than this Agreement; and (e) promptly notify Company of any unauthorized disclosure or use.

4. Non-Circumvention

For a period of three (3) years from the Effective Date, Recipient agrees not to, directly or indirectly:

  1. Contact, solicit, negotiate with, or enter into any business arrangement with any supplier, manufacturer, investor, customer, or other contact introduced by or identified through Company, without Company's prior written consent;
  2. Circumvent, avoid, bypass, or otherwise deprive Company of its interest in any transaction or opportunity that originated from or was facilitated by Company's Confidential Information; or
  3. Assist any third party in doing any of the foregoing.

Recipient acknowledges that Company's relationships and contacts are valuable business assets and that any circumvention would cause Company irreparable harm.

5. Intellectual Property

Nothing herein grants Recipient any license, right, title, or interest in or to Company's intellectual property, including the Quick-Change Rear System, any patent applications (pending or future), trademarks, or trade secrets. Disclosure of Confidential Information is not a waiver of any intellectual property rights.

6. Return or Destruction

Upon Company's written request, or upon termination of discussions under this Agreement, Recipient shall promptly return or certify destruction of all Confidential Information and copies thereof in its possession or control.

7. Term

Confidentiality and non-disclosure obligations survive for five (5) years from the Effective Date. Non-circumvention obligations survive for three (3) years from the Effective Date. Obligations with respect to trade secrets continue for as long as the information qualifies as a trade secret under applicable law.

8. Remedies

Recipient acknowledges that breach or threatened breach would cause Company irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate, and that Company shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief and specific performance without posting bond, in addition to all other remedies at law or in equity.

9. No Obligation to Proceed

Nothing in this Agreement obligates either Party to enter into any further agreement or business relationship. Either Party may terminate discussions at any time without liability under this Agreement.

10. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Florida, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles. Any dispute shall be resolved exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Florida, and each Party consents to such jurisdiction and venue.

11. General Provisions

  1. Entire Agreement. This Agreement is the complete agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions.
  2. Amendment. Amendments require a written instrument signed by both Parties.
  3. Severability. If any provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.
  4. Counterparts; Electronic Signatures. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original.

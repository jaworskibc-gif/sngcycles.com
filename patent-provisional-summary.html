2. Title of Invention

Integrated Convertible Panel System for Electric Motorcycles, and Extended-Geometry Frame for Tall-Rider Accommodation

This filing covers two related inventions developed together on the same platform: (1) a clamshell body panel system that provides full drivetrain access while maintaining conventional motorcycle appearance, and (2) an extended-geometry electric motorcycle frame designed to accommodate riders of significantly above-average stature.

3. Field of the Invention

This invention relates to electric motorcycles, specifically to: body panel enclosure and access systems for electric motorcycle drivetrains, and frame geometry designed for tall-rider ergonomics in the electric supermoto and dual-sport motorcycle category.

4. Background — Problem Being Solved

Problem 1: Access vs. Appearance in Electric Motorcycles

Existing electric motorcycles present a fundamental design tension: the electric drivetrain (motor, battery, controller, wiring) requires periodic access for service, charging connections, and component replacement. Current designs either (a) expose the drivetrain in a permanently "raw" state that lacks conventional motorcycle aesthetics, or (b) enclose it in fixed panels that require tool-based disassembly and reassembly for any access. No existing production electric motorcycle offers a rapid, integrated, tool-free access system that simultaneously maintains conventional gas-motorcycle appearance when closed.

Problem 2: The Tall-Rider Gap in Motorcycle Manufacturing

The global motorcycle industry designs to an industry-standard rider height of approximately 5'10" (178cm). Production electric supermotos and dirt bikes have seat heights ranging from approximately 28 inches (entry-level) to 37.5 inches (performance-spec). No production electric motorcycle offers an adjustable seat height exceeding 38 inches. Riders over 6'2" are systematically underserved — forced to ride bikes where they cannot place both feet flat, cannot maintain proper ergonomic position, and experience reduced control and comfort. This represents a significant underserved market segment.

5. Invention 1 — Integrated Clamshell Panel System

Description

The Integrated Clamshell Panel System is a body panel enclosure mechanism for electric motorcycles in which the front brush guard/number board assembly, the gas tank cover panel (battery enclosure cover), and the lateral side panels (radiator guard substitutes) are mechanically interconnected as a single integrated assembly. The side panels are mounted on rotating pivot points that allow them to open outward (clamshell motion) to expose the full electric drivetrain — including the battery pack, motor controller, motor, and associated wiring — for service access. When closed and latched, the integrated assembly presents the visual and structural profile of a conventional gas-powered motorcycle, including the appearance of a traditional gas tank in the central upper frame position.

Battery Enclosure Construction Method

The central battery enclosure cover is constructed using the following original process: (1) A salvaged Husqvarna motorcycle gas tank shell is sourced; the bottom is cut off, retaining the OEM top mounting points for direct frame attachment. (2) The stock Sur-Ron battery door is removed and its center is cut out cleanly, leaving a mounting ring. (3) A plaster compound is mixed from shop clay, water, and baking soda and formed over the modified battery door; the tank shell is then mounted on top, creating a seamless factory-quality transition. (4) The battery pack is designed to install at a specific angle, locking it in place by geometry alone — no latches or additional hardware required. This process produces an enclosure indistinguishable in appearance from OEM motorcycle bodywork. No documented prior art exists for this construction method.

Key Novel Elements

  • Rotating lateral panels: Side panels (occupying the radiator guard position) mounted on pivot points allowing outward rotation without tool use
  • Integrated front-to-side linkage: Front brush guard and number board assembly mechanically connected to the gas tank cover, which is in turn connected to the rotating lateral panels — forming one unified opening mechanism
  • OEM gas tank shell as battery enclosure: Salvaged motorcycle gas tank shell repurposed as battery cover — retaining OEM mounting points, profile, and appearance
  • Plaster-formed transition layer: Clay/water/baking soda plaster compound used to form seamless integration between modified stock battery door and tank shell
  • Angle-lock battery access: Battery installs at a specific geometric angle, locking in place without latches or straps
  • Rapid tool-free access: Full drivetrain access achieved without tools through the integrated rotation mechanism
  • Closed-state structural integrity: When closed, panels provide structural reinforcement to the frame and full weather protection to the drivetrain

How It Differs From Prior Art

  • Existing electric motorcycle panels are static, bolt-on components requiring tool removal
  • No existing production electric motorcycle integrates the front number board, tank cover, and side panels as a single rotating system
  • The visual simulation of a gas tank profile in an electric motorcycle using an integrated panel system is novel

6. Invention 2 — Extended-Geometry Frame for Tall-Rider Accommodation

Description

An electric motorcycle frame design incorporating extended frame geometry to achieve seat heights of 39–43 inches (99–109cm) in a performance electric supermoto or dual-sport platform, specifically engineered to accommodate riders of 6'0"–7'0" (183–213cm) stature with both feet flat on the ground at the rider's preferred setting, while maintaining motorcycle handling geometry and weight distribution appropriate for high-performance riding.

Key Specifications of the Embodied Prototype

  • Platform base: Modified Surron LBX frame, extended to 450cc-class proportions
  • Seat height at rider setting (6'7" / 200cm rider): 39 inches (99cm)
  • Seat height at maximum extension: 43 inches (109cm)
  • Adjustable range: 4 inches (10cm) of seat height adjustment
  • Suspension: WP XACT 4 forks (front), extended linkage rear
  • Target rider height range: 6'0" – 7'0"+ (183cm – 213cm+)

How It Differs From Prior Art

  • No production electric motorcycle exceeds 38" seat height
  • No production electric motorcycle is specifically engineered for riders over 6'4"
  • The 43" maximum seat height exceeds every known production electric motorcycle by a minimum of 5 inches
C4. An electric motorcycle frame assembly comprising extended geometry providing a seat height range of 39–43 inches, wherein the frame geometry is specifically engineered to accommodate rider stature of 6'0" to 7'0" or greater with both feet placed flat on the ground at the minimum seat height setting, while maintaining performance supermoto or dual-sport handling characteristics.

A separate provisional filing may be warranted for the E-520 Jackshaft Fabrication System — a dual-stage chain reduction system (15.44:1 total reduction; Primary: 12T→48T at 4.00:1; Secondary: 14T→54T at 3.86:1) engineered as a complete gas-to-electric conversion kit for KTM, Honda, Yamaha, and Husqvarna 250/450 platform motorcycles. This system is delivered as a pre-built assembly in a retail packaging system. The novel elements include the specific dual-stage reduction geometry, the integrated mounting plate design (3/8" mild steel, adjustable slotted holes), and the retail delivery system (pirate chest format including pre-built assembly, fabrication guide, and complete hardware pack).

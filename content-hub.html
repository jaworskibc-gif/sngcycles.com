🦈 Team Engagement Briefing

Why This Works

TikTok and Instagram both rank content based on early engagement. Comments in the first 30–60 minutes after a post goes live carry the most weight with the algorithm. When the whole team drops real comments right after Bryan posts, the platform reads it as "this content is sparking conversation" and pushes it to a wider audience automatically. This is free. It costs nothing except 2 minutes of your time per day. Done consistently, it compounds every single week.

Daily Roles — Every Person, Every Day
Bryan
Post the content. Reply to every comment within the first hour — even just one sentence. The founder replying is the single highest-signal thing you can do. Spend 10 minutes going to 3–5 other moto/electric bike videos and leaving real comments from the @sngcycles account.
Fernando
Comment on every SNG post within 30 minutes of it going live. Real sentences — react to something specific in the content. Also spend 5–10 minutes commenting on 2–3 posts from riders, builders, or moto brands from your personal account. Tag @sngcycles when it's natural.
Roy
Comment on every SNG post within 30 minutes. Ask a question or hype a specific detail — "That swingarm extension is wild, what's the geometry change?" type comments drive replies. Drop a share or repost on your own account when content is strong.
Patrick
Comment on every SNG post within 30 minutes. Engage with any responses from followers — continue threads, answer basic questions, keep the conversation alive. The longer a comment thread runs, the longer the algorithm promotes the post.
How to Comment Right
✓
Real sentences only. "🔥" does nothing. "That seat height is insane, never seen anything like it on an electric" moves the needle. The algorithm reads comment length.
✓
Stagger your comments. Not all 4 of you at the exact same minute — that looks coordinated and can trigger spam filters. Spread over the first hour naturally.
✓
Ask questions back. "What do you think the range is?" or "Would you rock the gold or the holochrome?" gets the audience responding, which compounds the signal.
✓
Comment on OTHER people's content too. Riding videos, moto builds, tall rider posts, electric vehicle content. Every day. This trains the algorithm on exactly who the SNG audience is and puts the brand in front of the right people.
✗
Don't comment on random unrelated content. The algorithm uses your engagement history to understand your niche. Keep it in the moto, electric vehicle, and builder world.
The Bigger Picture

Most brands at this stage have zero team engagement strategy. They post and pray. We have 4 real people who believe in this brand and can move the needle for free starting today. That is an advantage most funded competitors don't have.

Done consistently for 30 days — real comments, real engagement, real content — the algorithm starts doing the work for you. The goal is to build enough momentum that organic reach kicks in and the brand grows without a paid ad budget. That is 100% achievable from where we are right now.

2 MINUTES PER PERSON PER DAY.
Comment. Engage. Repeat. That's the whole job. Sharks don't coast — they keep moving.

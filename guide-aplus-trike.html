Note on Fox Float 3 EVOL RC2

The Fox Float 3 EVOL RC2 shocks ($2,800–$3,400 for the set of 4) are the single biggest line item in the build — and they're what make the ride quality what it is. You can substitute with a more budget-friendly shock and bring the overall build cost down significantly. The manufacturing package and dimensions are spec-agnostic for the shock mount points.

Important — Skill Level

This is an advanced build. Frame fabrication from DOM tubing requires MIG or TIG welding capability. If you don't weld yourself, you can take the dimensioned drawings to any fabrication shop and have the frame made. The full package is designed to hand off to a professional fabricator — exactly how the original was built. You direct the build; the shop executes from the drawings.

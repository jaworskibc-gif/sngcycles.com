### A — Founders Gold (default)
- Plastics: full **gloss gold** (shrouds, side, tail, front plate area)
- Canopy: **gold**, linear rails black/silver
- Graphics: `NO GAS HAULS ASS` black; `SNG` orange/white; optional `520E / 52kW`

### B — Factory Orange
- Plastics: **bright orange** main + **white** side accents or white fender
- Canopy: orange or white
- Graphics: black/white SNG; less gold text
- Best for: tall-rider / proof content, garage energy

### C — Stealth White
- Plastics: **gloss white** full set
- Canopy: white; black latch hardware
- Graphics: black + orange only (high contrast)
- Best for: clean product shots, “not a toy” hypercar

### D — Stealth Black
- Plastics: **satin black** full set
- Canopy: black; orange fin only
- Rims: **black** (gold optional)
- Forks: black or dark
- Best for: night shoots, aggressive brand

### E — Holochrome
- Plastics: **color-shift / chrome holo** panels
- Canopy: match holo or solid black
- Graphics: minimal — let finish speak
- Best for: drops, reels, “show bike”

### F — Gold / Black two-tone
- Mid body **gold**; front fender + rear fender **black**
- Canopy: gold
- Best for: contrast without losing Founders gold

### G — White / Gold canopy (signature split)
- Body **white**; **Kinetic Canopy gold** (battery story pops)
- Best for: explaining canopy + premium two-tone

### H — Husky heritage
- White plastics + gold tank/canopy + red front plate
- Best for: FC450 language, hybrid gas-tank canopy story

### Graphics research — how GasGas actually does lettering (do not freestyle)

Source: official **GASGAS MC 450F Factory Edition** product photography + GasGas rider forums (wordmark typeface).

| Fact | What GasGas does | What we do **wrong** if we freestyle |
|------|------------------|--------------------------------------|
| **Wordmark** | One compound brand: **`GASGAS`** (no space) | Stacked meme poster lines covering the whole side |
| **Typeface** | **Square 721 Bold Extended** family (ultra-wide square geometric sans; factory wordmark is stretched/italicized on the shroud) | Generic Impact / thick condensed “block” Instagram type |
| **Placement** | **Radiator shroud / mid side panel** only — sits in a **black angular geometric field** (chevron/wedge cut) | Giant text on battery case, tail, and entire bike |
| **Scale** | Wordmark is **wide but not full-bike height** — reads as factory vinyl/in-mold, not a billboard | Text taller than the seat / half the bike |
| **Graphic kit** | Full kit: black geometric wedges, white number-plate field, small airbox “G”, fork-guard decals, fender stripes — **not text alone** | Only huge white letters on plain black with no kit structure |
| **Finish** | In-mold / factory vinyl flush to plastic | Floating sticker look, uneven perspective |

### SNG application (locked)

1. **Base plastics:** matte/satin **black** GasGas MC silhouette  
2. **Wordmark zone:** same shroud position as factory **`GASGAS`**  
3. **Type:** Square 721 Bold Extended–style **ultra-wide** white letters (slight factory slant OK)  
4. **Copy:** **`NO GAS HAULS ASS`** — may sit as **one wide line** or **two lines max** inside the black geometric field (e.g. `NO GAS` / `HAULS ASS`) if the shroud needs it — **never** four random stacked blocks covering the side  
5. **Kit accents:** keep GasGas-style **black geometric wedges / chevrons** under or around the wordmark so it reads as a **factory graphics kit**, not a slogan sticker  
6. **No** GasGas logos, no Rockstar marks, no fake #51 unless we design SNG numbers later  

**Mockup rule:** always seed from a real GasGas MC photo or a real SNG chassis photo — never invent toy CGI lettering.

# SNG Legacy Memory Bank

Keep these points available while the visible system is rebuilt from scratch.

## Brand and Positioning

- Core line: `Shark Not Goldfish`
- Audience center: e-moto riders, builders, stunt culture, Florida identity, anti-generic moto merch buyers
- Tone: harder-edged, founder-built, rider-first, not polished lifestyle filler

## Best Product Strategy From Legacy Pages

- Sell a focused hero set instead of a broad catalog
- The strongest legacy plan centered on 5 hero SKUs:
  - Rider Team Colors Snapback
  - Florida Grown Tee
  - Megalodon Tee
  - Conversion Tee Series
  - Shut Up Get Out And Ride Hoodie
- Strong bundle logic:
  - hat -> tee upsell
  - tee -> hoodie upsell
- Clear CTA direction:
  - `Pick your rider colorway`
  - `Wear the Florida signal`
  - `Claim the Megalodon tee`
  - `Pick the conversion colorway`
  - `Step into the rider uniform`

## Offer and Funnel Lessons

- One product, one angle, one CTA
- Do not send traffic to a full-catalog experience first
- Collection-first sell path works better than a scattered storefront
- Shopify should be the main merch destination

## Strong Creative Angles Worth Reusing

- Identity / tribe
- Humor / slogan
- Founder / build proof
- Gift / impulse / bundle

## Useful Content Rules

- 70% founder/build proof
- 20% culture/community
- 10% direct product push
- Show real work, rider energy, and bike-adjacent context
- Better hooks are short and blunt, not polished

## Good Hook Territory

- rider identity
- bike matching / team colors
- aggressive shark imagery
- Florida pride
- gas-to-electric conversion humor
- anti-soft / anti-generic language

## Operational Targets From Legacy Pages

- 350 units in 90 days
- roughly 27 units per week
- roughly 4 units per day
- hero launch should be measured weekly, not treated like a passive store

## What To Avoid Going Forward

- too many static dashboard pages
- overlapping internal systems that say the same thing
- catalog sprawl
- generic ecommerce wording
- pretty-but-soft brand presentation

Rules

Traffic rules

  • Cold traffic should land on the hat or hero drop first.
  • Identity content should route into the matching tee page.
  • Warm traffic should be given hoodie upsell opportunities.
  • Do not send narrow creative into a broad store browse path.

Creative rules

  • Identity, humor, founder proof, or colorway. Pick one.
  • The CTA should match the product and the landing page exactly.
  • Merch should feel earned by the content, not forced into it.
  • Anything that reads generic gets cut.

Options 1 & 2 shown — these two work cleanest in print

My take

Option 1 (Wordmark) — Cleanest. Works at any size. Scales to a sticker, an embroidery patch, a tank badge. Nothing to misinterpret.

Option 2 (Motorsport Bar) — Same as 1 but with more motorsport energy. Very KTM. The orange bar becomes a signature detail.

Option 3 (Fin) — The shark without being obvious. Works if the fin shape is clean enough. Risk: could read as a hill or a wave to someone who doesn't know the brand.

Option 4 (Badge) — Best for tank badges, patches, numbered plates. Too complex for most digital use. Secondary use only.

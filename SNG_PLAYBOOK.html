Section 03
Brand
Voice

SNG has a specific voice. It's earned, not performed. Blue-collar. No fluff. A little cocky because it's warranted. Talk like texting your boy — not like a press release.

NEVER
"We are excited to announce..." / "We are proud to share..." / "Thrilled to introduce..." — this is corporate speak. SNG never touches it.
NEVER
Long sentences. If it takes more than 8 words to make a point, cut it in half.
NEVER
More than one emoji per post. The 🦈 is the only one that belongs here.
ALWAYS
Short punchy lines. One idea per sentence. Drop to a new line after every beat.
ALWAYS
Land on a brand line to close: Built not bought · No gas hauls ass · Florida-built, VIN-stamped · Shark Not Goldfish
ALWAYS
Lowercase energy is fine on TikTok. IG gets proper caps. Either way, the voice stays the same.

What Needs Machining vs. What You Buy
The Key Point

Only two parts need machining: the mounting plate and the jackshaft. Everything else — bearings, sprockets, chain, fasteners, hardware — is off-the-shelf. The guide includes the exact machinist instructions for both custom parts. Hand them to any machine shop. No follow-up questions. No back-and-forth.

6-Step Assembly Sequence
1
Receive Machined Parts — Inspect Before Assembly
Verify plate dimensions and shaft diameter before doing anything else. Check bearing bore fit on shaft. Full inspection checklist in guide.
2
Mount UCP204 Bearings to Plate
197mm center-to-center. Torque bearing mount hardware to spec. Do not fully tighten until shaft is installed and both chains are set.
⚠ Do not fully tighten bearing mounts at this stage.
3
Install Jackshaft Through Bearings
Slide 20mm shaft through both UCP204 bearing bores. Stage your sprockets on the shaft in correct position before proceeding — position spec in guide.
4
Set Secondary (Rear) Chain First — Always
Align the 14T→54T secondary stage chain before touching the primary. This is the critical step. Chain alignment sequence is documented in detail in the guide.
⚠ Critical: Secondary chain alignment first — every time. Primary chain alignment depends on this position.
5
Set Primary Chain — Then Lock Everything
With secondary set, align primary 12T→48T chain. When both chains are running true with correct tension, fully torque all bearing mounts and set screws to spec.
M8: 22 Nm · M10: 40–45 Nm · Set Screws: 10–12 Nm
6
Final Inspection & Installation
Rotation check, chain tension verify, torque confirmation. Mount to frame per build guide (compatible with Guide #001, #002, and #003 builds). Full installation notes in guide.

Additional Notes — WP Suspension & Brakes
WP Fork Conversion — Lewis Brakes Adapter

When the SNG builds were in development, no ready-made adapter existed for WP fork conversion with the Lewis Brakes system. SNG developed CAD drawings for the adapter at that time. Since then, Lewis Brakes has released a complete kit at $249 — and this is now the recommended path. Even if you fabricated the adapter yourself, you'd still need a special notched axle and different spacer. The math doesn't work for DIY. Buy the Lewis Brakes kit.

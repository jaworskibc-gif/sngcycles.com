This Non-Circumvention, Non-Disclosure and Confidentiality Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the date last signed below ("Effective Date") by and between:

Disclosing Party Complete Care Marketing Inc. ("CCM"), a Florida corporation, located at 173 Saratoga Blvd W, Royal Palm Beach, FL 33411, represented by Bryan Jaworski, Founder & Managing Member.
Receiving Party   ("Recipient"), located at
 

CCM and Recipient may be referred to individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties." In consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any and all information, data, materials, technology, concepts, systems, and know-how disclosed by CCM to Recipient, whether disclosed orally, in writing, electronically, or by demonstration, including but not limited to:

  • All electric vehicle designs, engineering specifications, schematics, fabrication drawings, build documentation, and prototype configurations, including all SNG supermoto and trike platforms;
  • All proprietary vehicle architecture, drivetrain configurations, motor and controller integration methodologies, suspension setups, and frame geometry;
  • All intellectual property, patent applications (filed or unfiled), trademarks, trade dress, trade secrets, and inventions, including the "Shark Not Goldfish" brand identity and all CCM vehicle concepts;
  • All business plans, financial projections, pricing structures, unit economics, licensing models, and investor materials;
  • All supplier relationships, vendor contacts, manufacturer agreements, and component sourcing methodologies;
  • All marketing strategies, content plans, brand development, product roadmaps, and launch timelines;
  • All proprietary source code, platform architecture, API integrations, automation systems, and software designs;
  • All client lists, customer relationships, dealer networks, partnership agreements, and distribution channels;
  • Any information marked as "Confidential" or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure.

2. Obligations of Recipient

Recipient agrees to:

  1. Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and protect it with at least the same degree of care used to protect Recipient's own confidential information, but in no event less than reasonable care;
  2. Not disclose any Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of CCM;
  3. Use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of evaluating a potential business relationship with CCM and for no other purpose whatsoever;
  4. Not reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or attempt to derive the design, architecture, or methodology of any SNG vehicle, technology, or system;
  5. Not attempt to replicate, recreate, or independently develop any product, service, or system substantially similar to any CCM proprietary design, vehicle platform, or build concept;
  6. Immediately notify CCM in writing upon discovery of any unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information;
  7. Ensure that all employees, agents, contractors, and representatives who have access to Confidential Information are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less restrictive than those contained in this Agreement.

3. Non-Circumvention

Recipient hereby agrees that it will not, directly or indirectly, circumvent, avoid, bypass, or obviate CCM's interests in any way, including but not limited to:

  • Contacting, engaging, or entering into any business relationship with any of CCM's suppliers, manufacturers, investors, sponsors, dealers, or technology partners introduced or identified through CCM, without CCM's prior written consent;
  • Attempting to independently develop, license, sell, or commercialize any product or service substantially similar to or derived from CCM's Confidential Information;
  • Sharing CCM's Confidential Information with any competitor, potential competitor, or any party that could use such information to compete with CCM;
  • Taking any action designed to deprive CCM of the economic benefit of any business opportunity identified through or in connection with CCM's Confidential Information.

4. Intellectual Property Ownership

All Confidential Information disclosed by CCM, including but not limited to all vehicle designs, build methodologies, brand concepts, proprietary systems, and all related technology and intellectual property, are and shall remain the exclusive property of Complete Care Marketing Inc. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as granting Recipient any license, right, title, or interest in or to any CCM intellectual property, except the limited right to evaluate such information for the purpose of a potential business relationship with CCM. Recipient acknowledges that CCM's Confidential Information was developed independently, and that CCM retains all rights thereto.

5. Term and Termination

This Agreement shall remain in full force and effect for a period of five (5) years from the Effective Date, unless earlier terminated by mutual written agreement of the Parties. Notwithstanding any termination, the obligations of confidentiality and non-circumvention shall survive indefinitely with respect to any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law.

6. Remedies

Recipient acknowledges that any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement would cause irreparable harm to CCM for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy. Accordingly, CCM shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance, and any other equitable remedies available, in addition to all other remedies available at law or in equity, including but not limited to monetary damages, attorney's fees, and court costs. The Parties agree that the prevailing party in any legal action arising from this Agreement shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorney's fees and costs.

7. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Florida, without regard to its conflict of law provisions. Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Palm Beach County, Florida.

8. General Provisions

Entire Agreement: This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous agreements and understandings.

Amendments: This Agreement may not be amended except by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

Severability: If any provision of this Agreement is found to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

No Waiver: Failure to enforce any provision of this Agreement shall not constitute a waiver of future enforcement of that or any other provision.

Counterparts: This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including electronic signatures, each of which shall be deemed an original.

Binding Effect: This Agreement shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the Parties and their respective successors, assigns, heirs, and legal representatives.

Why the CRF450 Frame?

The 450-class MX frame gives you things that other electric build platforms can't: full-travel MX suspension from the factory, a proven geometry designed for aggressive riding, and a parts ecosystem that's everywhere. Surplus frames and donor bikes are plentiful on eBay — and they're cheap because the ICE powerplant is gone or worn out, which doesn't matter to you.

Husqvarna plastics fit the extended geometry of this build better than any other brand tested. The lines clean up, the proportions are right, and the mounting points work with minimal modification. The exact trimming and hardware spec is documented step-by-step in the guide.

The Real Math

A 52kW electric motorcycle bought retail costs $12,000–$20,000+. This build hits that power level for under $9K in parts — documented, sourced, and priced in this guide. The knowledge is what you're buying. The parts are on eBay right now.

## 1. Lead with immersion

Start with full-bleed real photography or video, not renders or abstract branding.

## 2. Use editorial type, not generic moto spam

Big display headlines, restrained body copy, and tight hierarchy beat loud clutter.

## 3. Proof beats promise

Show telemetry, parts, tool marks, serial logic, and real garage evidence before making claims.

## 4. Sell allocation, not checkout

The bike should feel invite-only and scarce, not like a normal add-to-cart product.

## 5. One primary CTA per section

Every surface should push one main action: apply, reserve, or learn more.

## 6. Treat price as a feature

$20,000 is not something to apologize for. It signals seriousness, parts quality, and scarcity.

## 7. Use dark surfaces and controlled metal accents

Black, graphite, aluminum, and one hot accent create the right premium frame.

## 8. Keep motion subtle

Use counters, reveals, and hover states carefully. No gimmick-heavy UI.

## 9. Present specs like instruments

Technical values should feel like a machine interface, not a spreadsheet.

## 10. Explain why each number matters

Do not dump specs without context. Tie geometry, power, and hardware to rider benefit.

## 11. Sell against the fit problem

Tall-rider geometry is not a side note. It is one of the core reasons the machine exists.

## 12. Position against premium ICE buyers

Frame the buyer as Ducati, BMW, Husqvarna, or track-day adjacent, never bargain electric.

## 13. Never use e-bike language

Do not say e-bike, Surron, Light Bee, gadget, budget, or cheap power.

## 14. Keep the tone precise and aggressive

The voice should be evidence-first, cold, expensive, and certain, with controlled bite.

## 15. Founder sacrifice is part of the product

The hours, cash, failed prototypes, and garage reality help prove legitimacy.

## 16. Merch supports the machine

Apparel is a culture and cashflow layer, not the headline over the bike.

## 17. The site has one real job

Make a premium motorcycle buyer believe the machine is real, then move him toward reservation.

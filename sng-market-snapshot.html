Target Buyer — Who Actually Buys an SNG

Core buyer: 25–60. Ducati Motard or BMW rider. Not the young Sur-Ron crowd.  This was confirmed by the founder firsthand — after buying an electric bike and watching who actually showed up with $20k to spend. It wasn't 22-year-olds. It was the guy who already had a Ducati in the garage and wanted to know what was next.
Primary Target · Ages 25–60
The Ducati / BMW Rider
Currently rides or has ridden a Ducati Hypermotard, BMW GS, KTM Super Duke, or Husqvarna 701. HHI $100k–$250k+. Cares about brand identity, fit and finish, and what the bike says about him. Has looked at the electric options and been underwhelmed — a Sur-Ron looks like a bicycle, a Zero looks like nothing.

This buyer is waiting for someone to make electric feel like a Ducati. SNG is that answer. He doesn't need to be sold on electric — he needs to be sold on this electric.
Primary market · $19k–$40k purchase range
Secondary Target · Ages 30–60
The Tall Rider (6'2"+)
Underserved by every OEM on earth. Has spent 10–20 years riding bikes that never properly fit — knees too high, feet can't touch, ergonomics always a compromise. When this rider sits on an SNG with 39"+ seat height and both feet flat, the conversation is over. Extremely loyal. Extremely vocal. Tells every tall rider he knows.

Overlaps heavily with the Ducati/BMW demographic — these are often the same buyer.
~14M adults 6'2"+ in the US alone
Tertiary Target · Ages 28–55
The Collector / Off-Road Buyer
Buys limited-edition vehicles as both toys and investments. Already has a Can-Am or Polaris in the garage. Looking for something that stands alone — numbered, custom, hand-built. The Hammerhead and the Megladon Collector Box are built for this person.

Also includes the dune / trail rider who wants electric without the mass-market feel of a Polaris RZR.
$45k–$75k purchase range · SNG Motors

Production Economics — 30 Unit Run

Demo builds use retail single-unit sourcing. At 30-unit volume, key cost reductions:

Motors (direct China — Sotion/EBMX)–25 to –35%
Battery packs (custom China OEM, 30 units)–40 to –55%
Wheels (Warp9 blanket order)–15 to –20%
Wiring harness (Cloom Tech custom run)–30 to –40%
Plastics + wraps (volume pricing)–20 to –30%
Labor (learning curve efficiency)–25 to –40%

Estimated 30–45% total BOM reduction at 30-unit run. A $21,000 demo Megalodon becomes a $12,000–$15,000 production unit — supporting a strong gross margin at $35K–$55K MSRP.

Executive Action Brief Founder: Bryan Jaworski

Bike credibility first. Gear identity second. The 270E earns the apparel. "Fox and Alpinestars were built on gas. SNG is built for lithium." — Use this line.

First 30 Days — 5 Actions

  1. Shoot the 270E as the proof asset. Do this week Film 3–5 short clips: motor reveal, fork/billet hardware close-up, controller shot, tall-rider stance. These become the hooks for every post this month. No apparel posts without at least one bike frame.
  2. Set up the SNG Growth OS in Google Sheets. Free Four tabs: Post Tracker (hook, asset, platform, views, comments, saves, next action) — Creator CRM (account, platform, followers, outreach date, result) — Keyword Watchlist (Sur-Ron, Talaria, Stark, big-bike stance, tall rider, electric supermoto) — Lead Sheet (name, source, tag: apparel/270E/collab). This is the system before any paid tool.
  3. Launch on TikTok and Instagram with the 70/30 mix. Week 1 70% bike/build proof content, 30% gear. Post a build clip on TikTok first — sharp first frame, 3-second hook. Repost to Reels as a polished edit. Use IG Stories for hat vs. hoodie polls and tall-rider sizing questions. Target: 3 posts per week, every week.
  4. Drop the hoodie and hat as the first hero products. Week 2 Don't launch all products at once. Lead with hoodie and hat — photograph them on or beside the 270E, not a model alone. Create a Payhip drop page with color options, zoom, and sizing. Link in bio. Make a "DROP" comment trigger ready for Manychat (add this in month 2).
  5. Identify and engage 5 target creators this month. Free From the influencer watch list: follow Sur Ronster, GritShift, Charged Cycle Works, Talaria Boys, and one local garage builder micro-creator. Comment with real value — answer a question, add a spec, share a build angle. Do not pitch. Log every interaction in your Creator CRM.

Top 5 Influencers to Watch

  • 1
    Sur Ronster
    Largest e-moto audience. Stitch their popular clips with the 270E angle. Avoid associating with reckless rideout content — focus on build/spec comparisons only.
  • 2
    GritShift
    Hardcore parts culture. This is the most aligned community. Lead with billet hardware, brakes, and swingarm story. Comment with real spec knowledge — don't pitch.
  • 3
    Charged Cycle Works
    Parts educator audience. Use "clear proof language" — cite your controller, motor, and brake specs. Their audience asks questions SNG should be answering in comments.
  • 4
    Talaria Boys
    Comparison-focused content. Position SNG as the finished, tall-rider, lithium-built lane vs. their stock comparison framing. Use their comment sections for sizing/stance hooks.
  • 5
    Local Garage Builder Micro-Creators
    Seed hats and hoodies. Repost their garage proof with SNG gear visible. These reposts build credibility faster than any paid placement and cost only product + shipping.

Daily Driver — What to Open Every Morning

Open First
  • Metricool or Buffer — check yesterday's post metrics (views, saves, comments)
  • TikTok & IG notifications — any comments or DMs from the keyword accounts
  • Google Sheets Post Tracker — log yesterday's numbers, flag anything worth reposting
  • Keyword Watchlist — scan Sur-Ron, Stark, "tall rider," "electric supermoto" manually for 5 min
Do Next
  • Pick one post idea from the overnight trends or comment cluster
  • Run it through your Claude prompt library — generate 10 hooks, pick the sharpest first frame
  • Log any new DMs or comments that qualify as leads into the Lead Sheet with a tag
  • Reply to one creator comment with a real answer (not a pitch)
  • Add to Creator CRM if you discovered a new account worth watching
End of Session
  • Schedule the post (or queue it for shoot day if it needs new footage)
  • Update Post Tracker with hook used, asset, scheduled time
  • Flag any 270E or premium buyer leads for follow-up with right script
  • Weekly: pull metrics, note this week's winner and one thing to cut
3 Decisions to Make Before Every Post
  1. Bike first? If this post doesn't start with the 270E, a build moment, or a hardware close-up — it doesn't go out until it does. No standalone logo posts.
  2. What's the hook? Decide the exact first 3 seconds before you post. TikTok algorithm rewards replays — the hook earns the replay. Choose from your 10 generated options, don't wing it.
  3. What do I want them to do? Comment a trigger word ("BUILD," "DROP," "FIT"), click the link, DM for sizing, or save for later. Pick one CTA per post. Post it, log it, watch what happens.

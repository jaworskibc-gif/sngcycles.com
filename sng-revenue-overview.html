SNG Customs is the parent company housing two operating divisions — SNG Cycles (2-wheel) and SNG Motors (3+ wheel) — plus a cross-division digital products, parts, clothing, and customer experience business. All vehicles share the Surron LBX platform core. Revenue is diversified across high-margin vehicle sales, recurring digital product sales, parts brokerage, financing income, and a clothing brand.

Parts Builder — "Pimp Your Bike" Platform

What it is
Online configurator — customer selects parts, wraps, accessories for their SNG build. AR virtual try-on. See it on the bike before buying.
How SNG makes money
10% parts broker margin on every item sold through the configurator. Customer pays retail, SNG keeps the spread.
Financing
Klarna and Affirm integrated. Customer finances the parts build. SNG gets paid in full upfront. No carrying cost.
Ships together
All parts ship at once — one box, one delivery. No partial orders.
Build Poster
High-res poster of customer's configured build. With or without pricing. 5 photo angle choices. Delivered digitally.
Revenue model
Passive margin on every transaction. No inventory held by SNG. Scales infinitely.

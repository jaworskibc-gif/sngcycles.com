Why the SNG 270-E Spec?

The Light Bee is one of the best platforms in the small electric space — but it's a starting point, not a finish line. The SNG 270-E is what you get when a builder who has spent 14 months and $50K+ developing electric vehicles takes that platform apart and specs every upgrade that actually matters.

This isn't a list of bolt-on parts. It's a documented prototype build — the real SNG 270-E spec, built and tested. The guide is the blueprint for replicating it.

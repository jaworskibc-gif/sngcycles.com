2. Purpose

The Parties intend to explore a potential business relationship in connection with   (the "Purpose"). In connection with the Purpose, Company may disclose certain confidential, proprietary, and/or trade secret information to Recipient.

3. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any and all non-public information disclosed by Company to Recipient, whether orally, in writing, electronically, or by any other means, including but not limited to:

  1. Product designs, engineering specifications, schematics, blueprints, prototypes, and build documentation;
  2. Intellectual property, patent applications (filed or unfiled), trademarks, trade secrets, and inventions;
  3. Business plans, financial projections, pricing, unit economics, and investor materials;
  4. Customer lists, supplier relationships, vendor contacts, and distribution channels;
  5. The identity of any business partner, investor, manufacturer, or contact introduced by Company;
  6. Marketing strategies, brand development, product roadmaps, and launch timelines;
  7. Any other information that a reasonable person would consider confidential given the nature of the disclosure.

Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of Recipient; (b) was rightfully known to Recipient prior to disclosure; or (c) is required to be disclosed by law or court order, provided Recipient gives Company prompt written notice.

4. Non-Disclosure Obligations

Recipient agrees to:

  1. Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence;
  2. Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of Company;
  3. Use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose stated in Section 2 and for no other purpose;
  4. Limit access to Confidential Information to Recipient's employees or advisors who have a need to know and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less restrictive than those contained herein;
  5. Promptly notify Company in writing upon discovery of any unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information.

5. Non-Circumvention

Recipient agrees that, for the Term of this Agreement and for a period of   years thereafter, Recipient shall not, directly or indirectly:

  1. Contact, solicit, negotiate with, or enter into any agreement with any person, company, supplier, manufacturer, investor, or business contact introduced or identified by Company through this relationship, without the prior written consent of Company;
  2. Attempt to bypass, circumvent, or exclude Company from any transaction, business relationship, or opportunity that arose from or was facilitated by Company's introduction or disclosure of Confidential Information;
  3. Use Company's contacts, relationships, or proprietary knowledge to compete with Company or to divert business opportunities away from Company;
  4. Assist any third party in circumventing Company's business relationships or opportunities.

A breach of this non-circumvention clause shall entitle Company to all commissions, fees, and compensation that would have been due to Company had the circumvention not occurred, in addition to all other remedies available at law or in equity.

6. Intellectual Property

Nothing in this Agreement grants Recipient any license, right, title, or interest in any intellectual property owned by Company, including any patent, trademark, trade secret, copyright, or proprietary design. All Confidential Information remains the sole property of Company. Recipient shall not file any patent, trademark, or other IP application based on or derived from Company's Confidential Information.

7. Return or Destruction of Materials

Upon written request by Company, or upon termination of the Purpose or this Agreement, Recipient shall promptly return or certify the destruction of all Confidential Information in Recipient's possession, including all copies, notes, summaries, and derivative works.

8. Term

This Agreement shall be effective as of the date signed and shall remain in effect for a period of   years, unless terminated earlier by mutual written agreement of the Parties. The obligations of non-circumvention shall survive expiration or termination of this Agreement as provided in Section 5.

9. Remedies

Recipient acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement would cause irreparable harm to Company for which monetary damages would be inadequate. Accordingly, Company shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance, and any other equitable remedy without the requirement of posting bond, in addition to all other remedies available at law. In the event of a breach, Recipient shall be liable for all of Company's costs and reasonable attorneys' fees incurred in enforcing this Agreement.

10. General Provisions

  1. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Florida, without regard to conflict of law principles. Any dispute shall be resolved in the courts of Palm Beach County, Florida.
  2. Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior discussions and understandings.
  3. Amendment. This Agreement may not be amended except by a written instrument signed by both Parties.
  4. Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is found invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.
  5. No Waiver. Failure to enforce any provision of this Agreement shall not constitute a waiver of any right.
  6. Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including electronic signatures, each of which shall be deemed an original.
